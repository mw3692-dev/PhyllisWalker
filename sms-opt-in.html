Who Is in This Program

This program is for existing clients of the studio — people who have previously purchased from Phyllis in person. We build the list from our own client relationships and nothing else.

We do not purchase, rent, import, or text third-party or purchased phone lists. Every mobile number we message belongs to someone who has bought from Phyllis directly and told us they want to hear from us.

How to Opt In

You can join the text program in any of these ways. All of them require you to affirmatively agree — we never add a number on your behalf, and we never add a number simply because it appears on a past order.

  • In the studio, verbally. Most of our clients join this way. When you are in for a purchase or a fitting, Phyllis will ask whether you would like text reminders about appointments and notice of client events. If you say yes, we add your number and record the date and that you agreed in person. Your first message from us states how to stop, so you always have the opt-out in writing even though you agreed out loud.
  • In writing, in the studio. Complete and sign the paper sign-up sheet, or scan the QR code that opens our online form.
  • Online. Check the text-message consent box on our sign-up or booking form (shown below). The box is never pre-checked.

Every message includes a way out. However you joined — spoken, signed, or online — every text we send supports replying STOP, and we honor it immediately and automatically. If you ever receive a message you did not expect, replying STOP once ends them for good.

What Happens After You Opt In

You will receive one confirmation message:

“Phyllis Walker J. Hilburn Stylist Studio: You're confirmed to receive appointment reminders and client event news from us. Msg freq varies, msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.”

After that you will receive reminders about your upcoming appointments, occasional prompts to book your next fitting, and notice of client-only events — as described above.

How to Opt Out

Reply STOP to any message from us. Opting out is processed automatically and immediately. You will receive a single confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and then no further marketing texts unless you opt back in.

Opting out of text messages does not affect your status as a client, and does not unsubscribe you from email — those are separate consents, and you can manage each independently.
