Note on the $ figures: invoices are billed at the client level, not per RT, so there is no real per-RT dollar amount in the source data. "Est. Revenue" and "Est. Cost" are estimates only: hours worked × Billed Rate (source field: Partner Rate - the rate actually billed to the client) and hours worked × RS Current Rate (source field: Current Rate with Client - RS's internal cost rate for the RT, falling back to RS Base Rate when unset). RTs on a Fixed Rate billing arrangement (flat monthly fee, not hourly) are excluded from Est. Revenue for now; RTs with no Billed Rate on file show "—" instead of a misleading $0.
